BUILT TO PLAY FAIR

Your skill. Not the algorithm.

Every new trio has a way.

Before a deal reaches you, we search for an ordering and placements that fit all three, including line clears. Your choices still matter: a different placement can block the rest.

No score-based tricks. Ever.

The dealer never reads your score. Eight piece families start in shuffled bags, one of each family per bag. Orientations are fixed: blocks do not rotate.

Honest about the safety net.

Pieces that cannot make a solvable trio wait in the queue. After limited attempts, three single blocks rescue the deal. These exceptions affect actual frequencies; there is no promise of equal drops or a fixed drought limit.

Clear more. Score more.

Each block cell earns 5. Each line earns 100 × your consecutive-clear streak. Clearing multiple lines at once adds 50 per extra line. Rows and columns clear together.
